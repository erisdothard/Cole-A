# Cole-A — project rules

Read this before doing anything in this repo. It overrides habit.

## The job

A new website for Cole Anderson ("CVZ") — Nashville visual designer, screen printer, full-time DJ. Client of Eris. Not an edit of his Wix site.

The brief is `docs/client-brief.md`, in Cole's own words. **It is complete and it is not wrong.** Do not propose going back to Cole to "revisit" or "confirm" it. If a build scores badly against it, the build is wrong.

## The direction (as of 2026-09-17)

**Less computer, more TV. Old-school VHS / CRT television.** Eris's words after reviewing both builds.

Cole's own words, which this must satisfy: CRT monitors · early-internet interfaces · cyberpunk graphic design · **distorted video** · scan lines · low-res graphics · **strange system messages** · technology that is futuristic but slightly outdated · a POWER UP button that turns on a machine and **pulls the viewer inside it** · then a **constantly moving** environment where merch, video, artwork, music and the bio exist as **floating windows, files, transmissions, folders, screens, fragments** — not pages · immersive but the viewer **always knows where they are** and can get back to the work.

### Rejected — do not rebuild these
- A splash page with a power icon and "press to enter" text (phone branch). Amateur.
- Fake BIOS / terminal boot text. Falling character rain. Cyan-on-black "Matrix" look (phone branch).
- Windows-95-style draggable windows with `C:\` paths and a taskbar (phone branch).
- The print-shop / "raster engine" metaphor as the *world* (local concept). He said machine/TV, not press.
- Placeholder content of any kind. Fake merch names, `hello@example.com`, "NO SIGNAL" boxes.

### Keep — these worked
- The curved-CRT moment where text scrolls on the glass (phone branch boot) — the *feel*, not the BIOS copy.
- System messages that pop up like spam (phone branch toasts).
- Lock-to-focus: things are degraded/moving until you point at them, then snap clear (local concept).
- The machine re-colouring itself in the colours of the piece in focus (local concept).
- Persistent edge readout of where you are + a plain-list index + a URL per piece (local concept).

## The bar

**Iterate until the build scores ≥ 8/10 against the brief, line by line, before showing Eris.** One pass is not a deliverable. A reviewer "PASS" on craft is not the bar; the brief is the bar. Score every line of the brief 1–10, fix the lowest, re-render, re-score. If self-score is below 7 it is a task list, not a report.

Do not present menus, options, or "your call" when the direction is already set. Do not hedge. Execute.
