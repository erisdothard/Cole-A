# CVZ — Interactive Portfolio / Website Concept

> Client brief from Cole Anderson, relayed verbatim by Eris on 2026-09-17.
> Referenced but NOT yet in the repo: the Johnny Mnemonic trailer Cole attached, and two music videos he provided.
> The brief refers to "the button" and a "POWER UP screen" without describing them — an opening section may be missing.

The CVZ website should feel less like a traditional portfolio and more like entering a computer system.

Think CRT monitors, early internet interfaces, cyberpunk graphic design, distorted video, scan lines, low-resolution graphics, strange system messages, and technology that feels futuristic but slightly outdated.

Almost like the graphics from the movie Johnny Mnemonic. I attached the trailer.

Clicking the button triggers the transition into the site. The feeling should be that the viewer isn't simply opening a website — they are turning on a machine and getting pulled inside of it.

Once inside, the portfolio becomes a constantly moving digital environment.

Rather than having conventional pages for Work, About, Video, etc., the different parts of the portfolio exist as objects and information inside the computer. Merch projects, videos, artwork, music, and the bio can appear as floating windows, files, transmissions, folders, screens, or fragments of information moving through the system.

## Overall Feeling

The website should feel like CVZ exists inside its own digital universe.

The viewer enters through the POWER UP screen, gets pulled into the computer, and then discovers the work as they move through the system.

The important balance is between immersion and usability. The site can be weird, chaotic, animated, and experimental, but the viewer should always be able to understand where they are and easily get back to the work.

The ultimate goal is:

Don't make a portfolio that looks like a portfolio.
Make a computer that happens to contain your entire creative world.
